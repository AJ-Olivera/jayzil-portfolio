Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. This policy covers the Column3 mobile application only.

Summary

Column3 is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not sync to a server, shows no ads, and does not share your information with third parties. This page explains what that means in detail, including the permissions Column3 requests and how the Premium subscription is billed.

Information we collect

Column3 does not collect personal or usage data on our servers. Everything the app keeps — your chosen language and theme, reminder times, quiz answers, streaks, mastered verbs, and practice history — is stored on your device only. Jayzil Studios has no access to this information and cannot view, retrieve, or recover it.

Data security

Settings and progress totals are kept in Android's encrypted secure storage. The detailed practice history is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. On top of that, the data is protected by your device's own security, such as your lock screen.

Accounts and sign-in

Column3 does not require or offer account creation. There is no sign-in, and no personal identifier (such as a name or email address) is collected by the app.

Backups

Column3 does not sync data with Jayzil Studios or with any cloud service, and it disables Android's automatic cloud backup, so nothing is copied to any server. Backup and Restore, in the app's Settings, lets you manually export your data to a file in a location you choose and restore it later — for example, when switching phones. You can create and share that file only through your own device's share menu. This backup file is never sent anywhere by Column3 itself.

Premium subscription and payments

Using Column3 is free, including the full verb list, pronunciation, daily reminders, backup, and a quiz allowance that renews every 12 hours. The optional Premium subscription unlocks unlimited quiz questions, progress tracking, and exclusive themes.

Premium is billed and managed entirely by the Google Play Store: Jayzil Studios does not collect, process, store, or have access to your banking, card, or other payment information. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal, and can be viewed or cancelled at any time in Google Play's subscription settings or under Settings > Subscription in the app. If Premium ends, your progress data stays on the device; Premium features simply become unavailable until you subscribe again.

Third parties and analytics

Column3 integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind. The only third party involved is Google: the Google Play Store distributes the app and processes Premium subscription payments, and Google Play Billing confirms whether your Google account has an active subscription — see Google's own Privacy Policy for how it handles that information. Verb pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine; Column3 sends nothing on its own. No data is sold or shared with any other third party.

Permissions

Column3 requests very few permissions, each used only for the purpose described here: notification permission, requested only if you turn on the daily practice reminder, so it can remind you at the times you choose; and access to Google Play Billing, for the Premium subscription. None of these permissions give Column3 access to any other data on your device.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, uninstalling the app — or clearing its data in Android's settings — permanently deletes all Column3 data. Jayzil Studios keeps no copy on any server, so the data cannot be recovered without a Backup and Restore file you created beforehand.

Children's privacy

Column3 is intended for users aged 13 and over. If it is used by someone below the age of majority in their country, this must happen with the consent and under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. Because the app collects no personal data, no information about minors is collected.

Applicable law

Because Column3 does not collect, transmit, or process personal data on its own servers or on third-party servers — all information stays exclusively on the user's device — the obligations of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) regarding data processing agents do not apply to Jayzil Studios with respect to this data. This policy is governed by the laws of the Federative Republic of Brazil.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published on this page and in the app, with a new effective date. Continuing to use Column3 after a change means you agree to the updated policy.
