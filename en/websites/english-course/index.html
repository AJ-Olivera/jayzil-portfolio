What the English Course is

The English Course is an online study track split into five levels — from "verb to be" to advanced formal-register structures. Every lesson explains the grammar in Portuguese, with a translation next to every English example, and every example can be heard with one click, so you connect the writing to the pronunciation from the start.

After each lesson, a set of exercises grades your answers instantly, shows your score, and saves your best result — so you advance a level while seeing your real progress through the course.

What you get

  • Five levels — Beginner to Advanced, following the international CEFR standard (A1 to C1).
  • Explanations in Portuguese — every grammar rule is explained in Portuguese, with a translation next to every English example.
  • One-click pronunciation — hear any example from the lesson directly in your browser.
  • Instantly-graded exercises — several ways to practice (fill-in, ordering, drag-and-drop, listening, and writing), with immediate grading and scoring.
  • Saved progress — your best score on every lesson is saved to your account, available on any device.

FAQ

Do I need to create an account?

Yes. The course saves your progress and score per lesson, so an account with email and password is required. See the Privacy Policy to see exactly what information is stored.

How does payment work?

The subscription is billed monthly through Stripe, with a 3-day free trial before the first charge. Jayzil Studios never sees or stores your card data — that stays entirely with Stripe, an internationally-certified payment processor.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, at any time, directly through the subscription portal — no need to contact us. Cancelling stops the next charge and your access continues until the end of the period already paid for.

Who is the course for?

For Brazilians who want to learn English from scratch or reinforce what they already know, at their own pace — no class, no fixed schedule. All content is in Portuguese, so no prior English knowledge is needed to get started.
