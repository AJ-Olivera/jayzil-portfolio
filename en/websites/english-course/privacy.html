Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026. This policy covers only the English Course.

Summary

The English Course requires an account to save your progress across lessons and devices, and a subscription to unlock access to the content. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why, and which companies process payment and login on our behalf.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email and chosen password (the password is securely managed by Google's Firebase Authentication — Jayzil Studios never sees or stores your password in plain text). You can also choose a username, displayed within the course.

As you progress through the course, we save your progress per lesson: your best score on each exercise and whether the lesson was completed. We also store your subscription status (active, cancelled, in free trial, etc.) and a Stripe customer identifier, used only to link your account to your subscription — never your card data.

Payments

Subscription billing is processed entirely by Stripe, an independent payment processor. Jayzil Studios does not collect, process, store, or have access to your credit card data or other financial information — that information stays exclusively with Stripe. See Stripe's Privacy Policy to learn how they handle that information.

Where your data is stored

Your account and progress data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud), the authentication and database provider used by the course. Only you have access to your own progress — each account can only read and write its own data, never another student's. The one exception is your email and username, also stored in readable form so that, if you lose access, your account can be located manually to provide support.

Your theme preference (light or dark) is saved only locally in your browser (localStorage), and is never sent to any server.

Third parties involved

Two third parties process data on our behalf: Firebase (Google), for login and storing your progress, and Stripe, for subscription billing. No other third party has access to your data — the English Course does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind, following the same privacy policy as every Jayzil Studios product.

Your rights (LGPD)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018), you may at any time request: confirmation of what data we hold about you, access to that data, correction of incomplete or outdated data, and deletion of your account and all associated data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below — today this process is handled manually by our team, there is no automatic account deletion within the course.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your progress data remains saved, in case you choose to subscribe again in the future — cancelling the subscription does not delete the account. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

The English Course may be used by students of various ages. Registration and subscribing assume legal capacity to accept this policy; if the course is used by a minor, this must occur under the supervision and responsibility of a legal guardian, who is responsible for the information registered and for the course's use on the minor's behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published on this page, with a new effective date. Continuing to use the course after a change means you agree to the updated policy.

Applicable law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Federative Republic of Brazil, including the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018).
