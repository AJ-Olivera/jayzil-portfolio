The client and the challenge

Eliana de Souza is an SEO and GEO specialist (optimization for search engines and for AI answers) based in Curitiba, Brazil. She needed a website that does what she sells: being found, understood, and chosen. She also wanted to present her clients' results in a way that is more compelling, catching attention without turning into a report full of numbers.

The challenge was to turn traffic cases, charts, and screenshots into a clear, beautiful, fast experience, while keeping the identity she wanted to convey: professional, confident, and welcoming.

From briefing to final approval

We worked in continuous dialogue with the client, from the first draft to launch. Nothing was delivered "finished" without being seen, commented on, and adjusted by her.

  • Briefing — we gathered her differentiators, audience, cases, and questions, including how to display results with impact.
  • Visual reference — together we defined the visual language she wanted: a light background, a bold violet, and rounded shapes.
  • Design system — we turned those choices into reusable rules before designing the first page.
  • Versions and adjustments — every section, image, and animation went through review rounds with her, until we reached the result she wanted.
  • Final approval and launch — the site only went live with her "okay", at elianadesouza.com.br.

The aesthetic she asked for

The client wanted a clean, professional look that is easy to trust. We created a custom design system with color, spacing, and typography tokens, in light and dark themes.

  • Color — white and neutral grays as a base, a violet as the brand color, and a rare peach as an accent.
  • Typography — Montserrat for headings and Plus Jakarta Sans for body text.
  • Shape — pill buttons, rounded cards, and a single soft shadow.
  • Icons — a custom set of 27 outline icons, no emojis.
  • Accessibility — color pairs with a minimum 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.

Dynamic, without losing speed

We used modern tools to make browsing fluid and memorable, always respecting people who prefer less motion.

  • GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger — scroll-driven animations, like the "How I work" section that stays pinned while the steps appear.
  • GSAP SplitText — headings that enter word by word, with a mask.
  • Lenis — smooth scrolling, synced with GSAP.
  • Cases carousel — coverflow style, with each result's details in a modal.
  • Microinteractions — buttons with a shine, charts that draw themselves, and animated strips that pause off-screen.
  • prefers-reduced-motion — people who turn off animations in their system get the site without motion.

Built to be found

Since it is the website of an SEO specialist, the technical foundation had to be exemplary. The whole site is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no heavy frameworks, and includes:

  • Complete metadata, canonical URL, and Open Graph.
  • Structured data (JSON-LD): Person, ProfessionalService, WebSite, and FAQPage.
  • WebP images, with lazy loading.
  • A responsive layout, from phone to desktop.
  • A contact form with no server of our own, via Web3Forms.
